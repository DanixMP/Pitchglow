        Sing, see your voice, and make it sound the way you hear it in your head.

        Recorder draws your voice as a glowing line moving across piano-key lanes, so you can see exactly which note you are singing and how far off it is. Then it can auto-tune the take to the notes of any scale, from a gentle nudge to the robotic "Believe" effect.

        Around that grew a small studio: songs with a beat and several vocal tracks, harmonies (from intervals or from keys you play), voice characters, clean-up, effects, a mixer, a metronome and note editing. Everything you change is heard straight away, and exports sound exactly like what you heard.

        It's made for practising singing, writing and sketching songs, and for having fun with your voice, with no studio software, account or upload needed. It runs in the browser and can be installed as an app.

        1. Micyour voice into an AudioWorklet
        2. PitchYIN finds the period of each wave (100 times a second)
        3. Rollthe pitch line on piano-key lanes
        4. Auto-tunea correction curve pulls each note to the scale
        5. Voice enginePSOLA moves single waves to the new pitch
        6. Mixeffects, mixer and limiter, then speakers or a file

        Seeing your pitch

        The pitch detector (YIN) compares the sound with a slightly delayed copy of itself. The delay where they match best is the length of one wave: 11.4 ms is a lower note than 7.8 ms. Each lane on the roll is one key of a piano, so the line shows the note you sing.

        Auto-tune

        For every moment, auto-tune picks the nearest note in your scale and adds a correction to your pitch. The retune speed decides how fast that correction moves: at 0 ms it jumps (the robot sound), slower settings glide and let vibrato through.

        Changing the pitch without chipmunks

        TD-PSOLA cuts the voice into one-wave grains and plays them closer together (higher) or further apart (lower). The grains themselves aren't stretched, so your vowels keep their colour. Harmonies, voice characters and note edits all work this way.

        Real time

        A voice engine runs inside the browser's audio thread. While you sing it processes your voice with 25 ms of delay; during playback it renders each track from its grain plan, so every knob is heard on the next block and exports are identical to playback.

        Your songs

        Takes, songs and imported beats live in your browser's own database, and the settings in its local storage. Nothing is uploaded.

        Your voice stays on your device.

        • No accounts, no tracking. No analytics, ads, cookies or third-party scripts.
        • Recordings stay local. The microphone is only used while you press Listen or Record. Takes, songs and imported beats are stored in this browser's database (IndexedDB); settings in its local storage. Nothing is uploaded.
        • Files leave only when you export them (WAV, MP3, stems or a .recsong file), to wherever you save them.
        • One optional network request: "Load from GitHub" on the Profile page asks api.github.com for the author's public profile and avatar. GitHub then sees your IP address, like any website visit. The result is kept on this device, and "Forget the loaded details" removes it.
        • MIDI and microphone access are asked for by your browser and used only inside the page.
        • Offline: the installed app keeps its own files in the browser's cache so it works without a connection.

        Checking storage…

        To delete everything, delete your songs and takes in the app, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
